# Amazon Shipping Rates and Delivery Times

## Domestic Shipping (United States)

### Non-Prime Members Standard Shipping
- **Free Standard Shipping**: Available on qualifying orders of $35 or more of eligible items fulfilled by Amazon. Delivery transit time is typically 4 to 5 business days.
- **Orders under $35**: Standard shipping fees start at $4.99 to $6.99 depending on item weight and size.
- **Expedited Shipping**: 2 to 3 business days, starting at $7.99 + $0.99 per pound.
- **Priority / One-Day Delivery**: 1 business day, starting at $12.99 + $2.99 per pound.

## Calculating Shipping Times
- Delivery transit times begin when the package **dispatches**, not when the order is initially placed.
- Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding federal holidays. Weekend delivery is available in select metropolitan areas depending on carrier routing.

## Global and International Shipping (AmazonGlobal)
AmazonGlobal ships products to over 100 countries and regions worldwide.

### International Delivery Speeds:
1. **AmazonGlobal Standard Shipping**: 9 to 14 business days.
2. **AmazonGlobal Expedited Shipping**: 5 to 10 business days.
3. **AmazonGlobal Priority Shipping**: 2 to 4 business days.

### Customs, Duties, and Taxes:
- **Import Fees Deposit**: For international orders, Amazon calculates an estimate of customs duties, taxes, and fees ("Import Fees Deposit") during checkout.
- If actual import fees charged by border customs are lower than the deposit, Amazon automatically refunds the difference to your original payment method. If actual fees exceed the deposit, Amazon covers the excess—you are never charged extra.
