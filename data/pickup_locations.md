# Amazon Pickup Locations and Lockers

## What are Amazon Pickup Locations?
Amazon Pickup Locations offer a secure and convenient alternative to home doorstep delivery. Instead of shipping to your residence, you can select an automated Amazon Locker or a staffed Amazon Counter location during checkout.

## Amazon Lockers
- **Self-Service Kiosks**: Located at convenience stores, grocery stores, shopping malls, and transit hubs.
- **Accessing Your Package**: Once your package is delivered to the Locker, you receive an email/SMS with a 6-digit pickup code or a barcode to scan using the Amazon Shopping app via Bluetooth.
- **Pickup Window**: You have **3 calendar days** to collect your package from an Amazon Locker. If the package is not collected within 3 days, it will be automatically returned to Amazon, and a full refund will be processed to your payment method.
- **Size Restrictions**: Items must weigh under 10 lbs (4.5 kg) and fit inside dimensions of 16 x 12 x 14 inches. Oversized, heavy, or hazmat items cannot be delivered to a Locker.

## Amazon Counter (Staffed Locations)
- Located inside retail partner stores (e.g., pharmacies, convenience chains).
- A store associate scans your pickup barcode and hands you the package.
- **Pickup Window**: You have **7 calendar days** to collect your package from an Amazon Counter before it is returned for a refund.

## Cost and Availability
- Delivery to an Amazon Locker or Counter is available at **no extra fee**.
- Prime members receive their regular free One-Day or Two-Day delivery speeds to eligible pickup locations.
- Standard shipping rates or free shipping eligibility thresholds ($35+) apply for non-Prime members.
