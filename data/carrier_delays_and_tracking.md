# Carrier Delays and Shipment Tracking

## Why Shipments Get Delayed
While Amazon strives for on-time delivery, shipments may occasionally experience unexpected delays en route. Common reasons for delays include:
1. **Severe Weather and Natural Events**: Snowstorms, hurricanes, flooding, and extreme conditions can ground flights or halt ground transportation.
2. **Incorrect or Incomplete Address**: Missing apartment numbers, gate security codes, or misspelled zip codes.
3. **Carrier Transit Handoffs**: When packages transfer between Amazon Logistics and third-party partner carriers (such as UPS, FedEx, or USPS).
4. **Customs Processing**: For international shipments, customs inspections and border clearance may take extra processing days.

## Tracking Status Definitions
- **In Transit**: Your package is moving through the carrier network and is scheduled for delivery.
- **Out for Delivery**: The driver has loaded the package onto the delivery van and delivery is expected today by 9:00 PM.
- **Delayed in Transit**: The carrier encountered an operational or weather delay. A revised delivery estimate will display on the tracking page within 24 to 48 hours.

## Recommended Waiting Period (The 48-Hour Buffer)
- If your package has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, please allow an additional **48 business hours** for late transit scans to update.
- If the tracking status does not update after 48 hours past the original estimated delivery date, contact Customer Support with your Order ID to initiate an investigation, request a free replacement, or issue a full refund.
