# Missing and Lost Packages Policy

## What to Do if Package Shows "Delivered" But is Not Found
Occasionally, a carrier may scan a package as "Delivered" up to 24 hours before actual physical arrival, or place it in a discreet location to protect it from weather and theft.

Before reporting a missing package:
1. **Check Safe Drop Locations**: Look around porches, backdoors, side gates, mail rooms, garages, or with building management/reception.
2. **Check with Household Members and Neighbors**: Verify if family members or neighbors accepted the package on your behalf.
3. **Check for Attempted Delivery Notices**: Look for a yellow delivery notice slip left on your door or mailbox.

## The 48-Hour Resolution Window
- In most cases, packages marked delivered appear within **48 hours**.
- If 48 hours have passed since the "Delivered" notification and you still cannot locate the item, contact Customer Support with your 17-digit Order ID.
- We will immediately check GPS delivery coordinates with the driver and offer a **full refund or a free expedited replacement**.
