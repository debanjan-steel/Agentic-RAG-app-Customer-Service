# Amazon Prime Shipping Benefits

## Prime Shipping Options
Amazon Prime members receive exclusive delivery benefits on millions of eligible items fulfilled by Amazon.

### 1. Free Two-Day Shipping
- Available on millions of items across all 50 US states.
- No minimum purchase order required for eligible Prime items.
- Addresses in Alaska, Hawaii, and Puerto Rico may experience extended transit times (3 to 7 business days).

### 2. Free One-Day Delivery
- Available in thousands of eligible cities and zip codes on select items.
- Look for the "Prime FREE One-Day" logo on product detail pages.
- Order by the specified afternoon/evening cutoff time to receive items by 9:00 PM the following day.

### 3. Free Same-Day Delivery
- Available in select metropolitan areas on qualifying orders over $25.
- For orders under $25, a $2.99 delivery fee applies for Prime members (non-Prime fee is $9.99).
- Order in the morning (typically before 12:00 PM noon) to receive delivery by 9:00 PM the same day.

### 4. Amazon Day Delivery
- Choose a designated day of the week to receive all your weekly orders grouped together in fewer boxes.
- Available for most addresses with Prime membership at no extra charge.

### 5. No-Rush Shipping
- If you don't need your order right away, select "FREE No-Rush Shipping" at checkout.
- Earn promotional rewards or instant discounts to spend on digital products, eBooks, or future eligible orders once your package ships.

## Non-Eligible Items and Exceptions
- Items fulfilled by independent marketplace sellers that do not carry the Prime badge.
- Heavy and bulky items requiring special freight delivery (may incur an oversize delivery surcharge).
- Subscriptions and auto-deliveries (handled under Subscribe & Save schedule).
