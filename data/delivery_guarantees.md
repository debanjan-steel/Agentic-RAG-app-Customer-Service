# Amazon Guaranteed Delivery Policy

## Overview
Amazon offers guaranteed delivery on select items and delivery options. When guaranteed delivery is available on an order, it will be clearly indicated on the checkout page with the guaranteed delivery date and delivery time.

## Delivery Guarantee Terms
1. **Guaranteed Delivery Date**: If we provide a guaranteed delivery date on the checkout page, your order is guaranteed to arrive by that date.
2. **Cutoff Times**: Guaranteed delivery options depend on an order cutoff timer (e.g., "Order within 2 hrs 15 mins"). The order must be placed before the countdown timer expires and with the selected delivery speed.
3. **Eligible Items**: Only items displaying the "Guaranteed Delivery" message on the product detail page and checkout page are eligible.
4. **Address Requirements**: Delivery must be to an eligible physical address within the serviceable delivery zone. P.O. Boxes, APO/FPO addresses, and certain remote zip codes are excluded from delivery guarantees.

## What Happens if Delivery is Late?
If we provide a guaranteed delivery date and a delivery attempt is not made on or before that date:
- **Shipping Fee Refund**: Amazon will automatically or upon request refund any shipping fees associated with the order.
- **Prime Members**: If the late item was ordered using free Prime shipping, contact Customer Support. You may be eligible for a promotional credit or an extension of your delivery guarantee compensation.
- **Exceptions**: The guarantee does not apply if delivery is missed due to circumstances outside Amazon's reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disasters, strikes, customs delays, or if no one was available to receive the delivery when a signature was required.
